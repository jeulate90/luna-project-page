![Meet Luna](luna-nordvaekst-banner.png)

## What is Luna?

**Luna** is a football goals research bot powered by **NordVaekst.net**.

She helps people find and review football games that **could have a lot of goals**. Luna is made for goal-focused football fans who want a faster way to spot exciting matches, goal-friendly patterns, and games worth checking before building a slip.

Luna is not a magic tipster. Luna is a fun research assistant that helps you slow down, compare information, and avoid building slips completely blind.

## Example of how to use Luna 🧪

When Luna highlights a game, do not just blindly bet it.

Use it like this:

1. Read the Luna alert.
2. Check the teams on Flashscore or your preferred football stats page.
3. Look at recent scores, home/away form, lineups, and motivation.
4. Ask yourself: **Does the match script support goals?**
5. Keep the slip small.
6. Skip the game if the risk is not clear.
7. Never chase if the first idea loses.

Luna helps you find the game.  
You still decide if the game is worth your money.

---

## Simple research checklist 🔍

Before placing anything, check team form, match context, home and away patterns, lineups, odds movement, and whether the market still makes sense.

## What Luna is NOT 🚫

Luna is **not**:

- ❌ A guaranteed winning system
- ❌ Financial advice
- ❌ A reason to bet more money
- ❌ A reason to chase losses
- ❌ A replacement for your own research
- ❌ A promise that a match will have goals

Football is unpredictable. A perfect-looking game can still end 0-0.

## Why Luna exists 💡

A lot of people build football slips too fast.

They see a few odds, add teams quickly, and hope for the best. Luna exists to slow that process down and make goal research easier.

Luna’s mission:

> **Help football fans discover goal-friendly games, research them better, and reject more weak slips.**

Sometimes the best decision is not to bet.

A pass can be a win.

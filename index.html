🤖⚽ Luna Project README

Meet Luna 🔥

Luna is a Telegram football goals bot that helps you spot football games that could have a lot of goals. Telegram is where you get the daily alerts, bet-slip research ideas, no-bet notices, and the free companion eBook/PDF guide. Discord is where the community discusses matches in cleaner channels.

What Luna does 📈

⚽ Goal-focused alerts • 🔥 Big-match vibes • 🤖 Bot-powered research • 🚦 No-bet warnings • 📘 Companion guide • ✨ Cleaner decisions

Luna helps you find games worth checking, but you still make the final decision.

Free companion eBook/PDF guide 📘

The guide helps users understand Luna messages, review goal-market context, compare football information, avoid forced slips, and build decisions with more discipline.

The guide is available through the Telegram community, not as a public direct download from this GitHub page.

Telegram and Discord 💬

Telegram: daily Luna alerts, possible bet-slip research ideas, no-bet notices, and the companion guide.

Discord: match discussion, questions, community talk, and cleaner channel-based conversation.

Research checklist 🔍

Check form, home/away patterns, lineups, odds movement, match motivation, and whether the game script actually supports goals.

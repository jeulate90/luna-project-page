Meet Luna 🔥

Luna is a bot that tells you about football games that could have a lot of goals. She helps goal-focused football fans spot exciting matches, check context, and avoid building slips completely blind.

Research checklist 🔍

Check form, home/away patterns, lineups, odds movement, match motivation, and whether the game script actually supports goals.

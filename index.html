Project README - public information page

Luna helps football fans research goal markets.

Luna is a football goals research bot that scans fixtures and sends clean Telegram summaries. It is built for people who want more context before building a slip - not fake guarantees, hype, or "sure win" promises.

What Luna does

✓
Scans fixtures.
Luna reviews football matches and looks for goal-market research context.
✓
Sends clean Telegram summaries.
The output is quick to read, not a messy wall of numbers.
✓
Helps you decide what to review.
Luna can point attention to matches, but the final decision is always yours.
✓
Avoids repeated spam.
The bot uses duplicate-slip protection so the same main slip is not sent over and over.

What Luna is not

!
Not guaranteed profit.
No bot can promise guaranteed wins. Betting always has risk.
!
Not financial advice.
Luna is research and education only.
!
Not a reason to chase losses.
If a slip loses, stop, review, and protect your bankroll.

Why this project exists

Most people build bet slips too fast: they see odds, add teams, and hope. Luna exists to slow that process down and give people a cleaner research layer before they act.
